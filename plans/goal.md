## Acceptance sequence

1. Bounded site surveys are implemented with limited cross-API isolated evidence. Complete altar boundaries and independent material/permission fixtures remain open.
2. A single-cell placement, exact whole-capture read-back, undo and cold reload passed. All 512 baseline voxels matched after reload; operation replay did not duplicate work, and old session tokens/native receipts were refused.
3. Version 0.9.1 implements bounded floor/wall/doorway application with stable logical keys, session binding, durable intent and one retained native receipt. Up to 64 cells must fit one aligned brick. The 16-cell live plan proof remains pending. These are bounded synchronous transactions, not a general job/revision system.
4. Measure wall, corner, doorway, stair, roof, storage and workstation modules. Check rotations, clearances, traversal and interactions visually.
5. Build a usable two-storey workshop with stairs, roof, doors, storage and workstations. Verify appearance, interaction, applied persistence and recovery.

Keep every proof quiescent and isolated. Placement has no native compare-and-swap or atomicity guarantee. Native multi-change application may absorb a foreign edit into its recorded postimage; strict multi-cell undo additionally requires the entire expected voxel image. This does not establish general concurrent safety. Receipts are session-only, tombstones refuse replay after restart, and no stateful recovery is provided. A geometric capture does not preserve all prop contents.

Recipe alternatives, unlock reads, eligible stock and survival debit/refund remain separate research. The broader knowledge and mechanics goal remains open.
