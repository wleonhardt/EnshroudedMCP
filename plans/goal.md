# Administrative construction

Status: open. Administrative plan spawning is selected before survival accounting.

Deliver survey, preview, apply, verify and recovery workflows for advanced structures through published enshwick APIs. Administrative mode makes no survival-cost, inventory debit or progression-feasibility claim. Preserve write gates, game-thread scheduling, bounded work, journaling and protected-region checks.

## Acceptance sequence

1. Read bounded site geometry, materials, altar boundaries and build rights. Compare known fixtures and distinguish unloaded data.
2. Place one tiny fixture behind the gate, journal durable intent and transaction identity, reconcile status, read back, undo and compare the baseline. Verify save/reload.
3. Add stable plan/element keys, world/session binding, idempotent revisions and bounded jobs. Repeating or interrupting a request must not silently duplicate structures.
4. Measure wall, corner, doorway, stair, roof, storage and workstation modules. Check rotations, clearances, traversal and interactions visually.
5. Build a usable two-storey workshop with stairs, roof, doors, storage and workstations. Verify appearance, interaction, persistence and recovery.

Framework transaction APIs support geometry edits, captures, status and undo in source. They are not yet live acceptance evidence for this MCP. Undo is bounded, session-scoped and can conflict. A geometric capture does not preserve all stateful prop contents.

Recipe alternatives, unlock reads, eligible stock and survival debit/refund remain separate research. The broader knowledge and mechanics goal remains open after a building benchmark passes.
