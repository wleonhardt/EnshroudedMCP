Larger structures and persistence while these layers are applied remain unverified. Accepted undo remains strict, another unsettled operation blocks it, and queued undo revokes settlement until baseline verification.
5. Measure wall, corner, doorway, stair, roof, storage and workstation modules. Check rotations, clearances, traversal and interactions visually. Initial rendered 0.10 cell trials are narrow appearance observations, not module acceptance.
6. Build a usable two-storey workshop with stairs, roof, doors, storage and workstations. Verify appearance, interaction, applied persistence and recovery.

Keep every proof quiescent and isolated. Placement has no native compare-and-swap or atomicity guarantee. Native multi-change application may absorb a foreign edit into its recorded postimage; strict multi-cell undo additionally requires the entire expected voxel image. This does not establish general concurrent safety. Receipts are session-only, tombstones refuse replay after restart, and no stateful recovery is provided. A geometric capture does not preserve all prop contents.
