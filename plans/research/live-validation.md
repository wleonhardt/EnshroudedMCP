# Public validation scope

The dated [isolated evidence](2026-11-13-live-validation.md) records narrow semantic observations. It does not prove general mechanics, complete crafting eligibility or construction acceptance.

The neutral-ID 0.7.0 publication snapshot passed 101 Rust tests, 24 Python helper tests, Clippy with warnings denied, formatting checks and a release build. These are offline checks. Installation and live acceptance of its neutral module IDs are separate and have not been performed. The new survey has exact synthetic cell-order/material/fill tests and admission probes; successful live reads, persistence boundaries and rights comparisons remain separate acceptance requirements.

Raw reports, game-derived catalogues, logs and deployment details remain private and excluded.
