# Public validation scope

The dated [isolated evidence](2026-11-13-live-validation.md) records narrow semantic observations. It does not prove general mechanics, complete crafting eligibility or overall construction acceptance.

The 0.11.0 publication snapshot passed 125 Rust tests, 27 Python helper tests, Clippy with warnings denied, formatting and a release build. The original research-ID isolated 0.11.0 deployment passed all 18 settled fixed read-only probes. Neutral publication module IDs have not been installed or tested live.

One quiet single-cell proof passed exact whole-capture placement/read-back/undo. Lost-response reconciliation and repeated operation identity did not duplicate transactions. After cold reload, all 512 baseline voxels matched; stale session tokens and old native receipts were refused. This establishes the reverted fixture, not persistence of an applied structure, full stateful restoration or general concurrency safety.

Version 0.10.0 implements up to 256 keyed planned cells across eight aligned bricks, at most 64 cells per brick. The 16-cell single-brick floor live proof and post-undo cold reload passed; the 32-cell two-brick proof passed placement/read-back/replay/undo with exact captures; its final post-undo cold reload passed with all 1024 baseline cells equal. The framework has no placement compare-and-swap or atomicity guarantee and may absorb foreign edits during multi-change application. Strict multi-cell undo requires exact expected voxels in addition to unchanged props, persistence and the native conflict check. All proofs require a quiescent isolated site.

Earlier bounded survey evidence compared separate voxel samples with sampled survey cells and observed whole-box persistence. That check does not prove complete protection boundaries, independent material semantics, connected-player permission behavior or applied save/reload persistence.

Raw reports, game-derived catalogues, logs, session receipts and deployment details remain private and excluded.

Undo restored all 512 baseline cells. The session-created flag was removed.

After coordinated logout and two empty checks, a 21-file cold save/ledger archive passed integrity and SHA256 verification. Reload restored all 512 baseline cells. The plan identity stayed stale without reconstructing its native transaction; old-session replay was refused with a fresh gate, and ledger hashes stayed unchanged. The temporary flag was removed. An initial startup stale-state assertion failed without capturing the returned state; a subsequent direct observation and complete rerun passed. Startup readiness requires a settled hosted-world observation; a reachable listener alone is insufficient.

This proves a controlled bounded floor and post-undo persistence. A separate applied-floor checkpoint subsequently matched all 512 captured voxels after cold reload without reverting the fixture. This is narrow voxel persistence evidence, not appearance/usability, general concurrent safety, stateful recovery or advanced workshop completion. Next: the bounded two-brick fixture and measured reusable modules. The current implementation is not a general resumable job or cancellation system.

Lost-response reconciliation, canonical replay without another commit, changed-request refusal, guarded undo and duplicate no-ops passed. No generalized concurrency, stateful recovery or workshop usability claim follows.

The fixed read-only validator waits boundedly for a hosted-world state when an operation-status probe reports an inactive session during startup. Listener availability alone is not readiness; unrelated errors and terminal unknown outcomes are not retried.

Final two-brick acceptance includes a verified cold archive, all 1024 baseline cells equal after reload, refusal of stale IDs/tokens behind a fresh gate, unchanged ledger hashes and an absent session flag. ## 0.11 acceptance scope

The isolated research-ID 0.11.0 deployment passed 18 settled fixed read-only probes after a verified cold archive. The validator requires the known twenty-one-tool surface but never invokes `building_accept` or other writes, even when advertised. Its broader verdict remains incomplete. Neutral publication IDs remain untested live.

`building_accept` has offline coverage and one isolated staged live fixture. It requires a native Applied transaction, exact current captures, the sole original actor, rights, persistence and a healthy journal. A synced acceptance record precedes interlock release. Acceptance retains the session-native receipt and is not a save/reload claim. Duplicate acceptance does not append another record. Accepted undo requires the strict postimage even for one cell, is blocked by another unsettled operation and revokes acceptance/settlement when queued until exact baseline verification. On 2026-11-14, the five-cell frame plus one-cell filler passed staged acceptance and layering under the quiescent-site limits.

Rendered 0.10 experiments observed one cell appear and disappear on undo. Separate five-cell-gap and six-cell-solid trials compared appearance; they did not prove incremental filling between accepted layers. A sampled air cell above terrain rendered a building patch contacting grass visually, then undo restored the exact baseline. These observations do not establish a general material palette, collision/traversal behavior or workshop usability. External desktop control used for observation is not part of this repository; no screenshots or controller patches are included.

## Staged acceptance and gap-fill checkpoint

On 2026-11-14, an isolated five-cell frame and a separate one-cell gap filler passed six staged phases. Each phase matched its expected entire 512-cell capture. Acceptance/replay guards passed. Wrong-order undo was refused without poisoning the retained receipt; reverse-order undo restored the exact baseline. The rendered gap closed after filling, reopened on filler undo and disappeared on frame undo. This is actual incremental same-brick layering evidence for this small fixture, unlike the earlier separate gap/solid trials.

A 31-file cold archive passed CRC/integrity and SHA-256 verification. After reload, all 512 baseline cells matched. Both old native IDs were not reconstructed; dry and gated stale accept/undo requests were refused. An old preview token could not reserve a fresh identity. Ledger hashes remained unchanged and the session flag was absent.

This checkpoint proves the reverted small fixture and post-undo persistence, not persistence while these layers are applied, general concurrency safety, stateful recovery, the maximum plan limits or a functional workshop. The separate applied-floor persistence finding remains a different fixture. Neutral publication module IDs remain untested live, and the broader goal remains open.
