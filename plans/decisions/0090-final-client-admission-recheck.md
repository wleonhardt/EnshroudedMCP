Date: 2026-11-17. Status: companion source 0.21.1 qualified; installed companion 0.21.0 remains unchanged and native acceptance is pending.

The consumer checks permission and expiry after durable intent, then reads a final copied snapshot and constructs the command. The getter can return late or the operator can remove the write flag during that call. Previously neither condition was rechecked before setting native_submitted and calling the SDK.

Even with the deadline fix present, it also fails with native_submitted true. These failures prove prohibited SDK attempts, not successful game mutations; the fake independently refuses the commands.
