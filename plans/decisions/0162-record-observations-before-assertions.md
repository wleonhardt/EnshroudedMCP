The helper does not normalize an error into empty/healthy data or select a new tool/profile.

An initial recorder OSError refuses before the action. An intent without a reply is incomplete observation, not proof of execution, cancellation, exit/save completion or permission to replay.

Client-level parsing may already have discarded a malformed envelope; this helper cannot reconstruct it. Valid native MCP refusal bodies are retained unchanged.
